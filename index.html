Happy Monthsary, my love

· ✦ ·

For us, through everything

My love,

Two months of us, and every version is my favorite.

Loving you feels like finding freedom in a world that sometimes asks too much of me. There’s something about the thought of you that makes everything feel a little lighter, as if even from a distance, you give me somewhere to breathe. And whenever I’m with you, I feel free to simply exist, without having to figure everything out. It’s a kind of peace I never knew I needed, and somehow, I’ve found it in you.where I can simply be

This month hasn’t exactly been kind to us. We’ve both had our own battles, our own worries, and days when things felt a little heavier than usual. And although we haven’t been fighting each other, we’ve been fighting through things of our own. But what stays with me is how, through it all, we never forget to let each other in. No matter how difficult something gets, we still find our way to tell each other, to listen, and to remind one another that we don’t have to carry everything alone.through it all, together

I think that’s when I began to understand something about us: love isn’t necessarily the absence of difficult days. Sometimes, it’s having someone who makes those days a little less lonely. It’s knowing that even when life gets overwhelming, I have you to turn to, and you have me, too. And I wouldn’t trade that kind of love for anything.

What I hold onto

  1. The way you make even my most ordinary days feel worth remembering.
  2. How, even when life gets difficult, we never forget to make time for us.
  3. The comfort of knowing I can tell you anything, even the things I struggle to put into words.
  4. That we don’t have to have everything figured out to keep choosing each other.

And another thing I find beautiful about us is that no matter how much life demands from us, we still find little spaces in between to be together. Even when our days are full, even when our minds are somewhere else, we make time for the little conversations, the laughter, and the moments that belong only to us. I know it may seem like something small, but to me, it means more than you realize.

I also want you to know how proud I am of you. Even in a month that asked so much of you, you kept working toward what matters to you, and your recent accomplishments are proof of that. None of it happened by accident. It came from your effort, your patience, and the way you keep going even on the days you’re tired. I’m so happy for you, and I feel lucky to be the one cheering for you up close.

I don’t expect every month to be perfect, and I don’t expect us to have all the answers whenever life gets difficult. There will be days when things aren’t as light as we’d like them to be. But I hope we never lose this part of us: the willingness to communicate, to understand, to make room for each other, and to remember that we’re on the same side.

Two months may not seem like a long time to some, but I’m grateful for every little thing we’ve shared within it. For the good days, the difficult ones, and all the ordinary moments in between. If there’s one thing I want you to know, it’s that I’m not only happy to be with you when life is gentle. I want to be here when it isn’t, too.

In every lifetime, I’d still choose you.

Yours, always.

Even in your quietest struggles, my heart will always make room for you.
